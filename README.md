# Yardline // Depot Nine — a threejs-fps-02 entry

A complete round-based tactical FPS in the browser (Three.js). You are a Warden defending a two-site depot against Ravagers who plant a charge. First to 7 rounds, max 12. Buy phase economy (5 weapons, kevlar, grenades), 4v5 bot teams with waypoint pathing, vision/hearing, a team director (site splits, plants, retakes, defuse channels), hitscan gunplay with headshot multipliers and distance falloff, full HUD and synthesized WebAudio SFX. Fixed-step 60 Hz simulation, fully offline.

Controls: WASD, mouse look (pointer lock), click fire, R reload, Shift crouch, Space jump, E defuse, B buy menu.

Run: `node main.ts` (builds the dossier), or `node serve.ts` for the dev server.
